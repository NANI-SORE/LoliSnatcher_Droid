## Device and interactive test procedure

Use a disposable app profile and media directory with two sources (including two paths/accounts on one host), a merged tab, Favourites, Downloads, and an empty group. Exercise:

1. Edit global/source settings, including an explicit override equal to global. Restart; rename/collide/import profiles. Confirm drawer ordering and overrides survive.
2. Fetch several pages, hide all items on an intermediate page, jump backward/forward, stop/retry, switch during restoration, and change one secondary source's page size. Confirm selection and unaffected tabs remain stable.
3. Apply global/source/local-view Hide/Blur/Mark rules; metadata predicates, AI/flag filters, suspension and expiry. Reveal/restore an item, then edit the matching rule. Verify grid, preview, gallery, comparison and unattended discovery agree.
4. Download media with a misleading extension and provider-renamed filename; go offline, restart and view image/video/thumbnail/hidden items/comparison. Switch directories and overwrite during thumbnail generation; originals must survive cache clearing.
5. Queue overlapping batches, pause/resume/cancel/retry, interrupt network and kill/restart the process. Verify cap/dedup, no stale attempt publication, offline flags, receipt acknowledgement and notification navigation. Repeat Android SAF and iOS background/Photos flows.
6. Share multiple ordered items while switching tabs; cancel/retry and simulate unknown-length progress. Exercise desktop clipboard and Android/iOS receiving apps after the sheet closes. Confirm selection belongs to the captured tab and downloads continue independently.
7. Exercise split/slider/fade/flicker/difference/heatmap/data comparison modes, quality/resolution controls, filter changes, repeated close/reopen and cancellation. Pan/zoom huge static images and animations under memory pressure; monitor tracked budgets and originals.
8. Export/import each category and full packages with DB enabled and disabled. Include groups/empty groups/pages, rules, presets and conflicting profiles. Interrupt/fail restore and restart; ensure disk and live state roll back together. Test old journal recovery, scheduled/update backups and security-scoped iOS directory access.
9. Transfer between two real devices, including VPN/firewall/local-network denial, phrase rejection, cancellation and interrupted/replayed data. Verify category selection, profile remapping and completion history.
10. On macOS, resolve Pods/build/sign iOS and inspect localized permissions, Scenes, Photos, document picker, local networking and region decoder lifecycle. Build explicit embedded/no-GMS and no-Cronet Android variants and verify their transport behavior.
